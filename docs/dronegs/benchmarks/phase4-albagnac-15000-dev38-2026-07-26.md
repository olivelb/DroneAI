# Phase 4 — Albagnac 15,000-step quality run (dev38)

Date: 2026-07-26  
Trainer: `0.5.0-dev.38`  
Profile: `dev38-staged-rotation008-absgrad050-fastgs`  
Disposition: successful long-run quality validation

## Protocol

- existing read-only Albagnac dense COLMAP product;
- 1,376 Mavic 3E RTK views;
- deterministic `scene_index % 8 == 0` held-out split:
  1,204 training views and 172 held-out views;
- 15,000 optimizer steps, seed 42;
- progressive SH degree 0 to 3, one degree every 1,000 steps;
- resize factor 4, maximum width 1,600;
- maximum 1,500,000 live Gaussians;
- dev.38 FastGS-compatible renderer and AbsGrad-guided MRNF;
- lossless held-out target/prediction export followed by exact-pair
  LPIPS 0.1/AlexNet on all 172 views.

No COLMAP extraction, matching, mapping, or bundle adjustment was rerun.

## Comparisons

| Control | PSNR | SSIM | LPIPS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Exact LichtFeld PLY, same 172-view dev.38 evaluator | 18.900364 | 0.428674 | — |
| DroneGS dev.38, 1,200 steps, SH0 | 19.157091 | 0.440746 | — |
| DroneGS dev.38, 1,000 steps, SH0 | 18.963013 | 0.428099 | 0.625404 |
| DroneGS dev.38, 15,000 steps, SH3 | **20.820227** | **0.567601** | **0.434652** |

Against the exact same-split LichtFeld PLY oracle, the 15,000-step model gains
`+1.919863 dB` and `+0.138927 SSIM`. Against the 1,200-step dev.38 run, it
gains `+1.663136 dB` and `+0.126854 SSIM`. Its LPIPS is 30.50% lower than the
1,000-step dev.38 result.

This establishes long-run output-quality superiority on the identical
DroneGS split. It does not establish quality-to-time parity with LichtFeld:
the LichtFeld artifact used 500 optimizer steps, SH0, and its published
`21.068552 / 0.631048` report uses a different held-out ordering.
